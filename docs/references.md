# Statistical and Methodological References

**Project:** Social Science Statistics Fundamentals  
**Academic Level:** Graduate / Master's Level  
**Research Fields:** Economics, Political Science, Public Policy, Political Economy, and Related Social Sciences  
**Citation Style:** APA 7th Edition  
**Status:** Bibliographic Foundation — Version 0.1

---

## 1. Purpose

This document establishes the bibliographic foundation of the Social Science Statistics Fundamentals project.

The references support the theoretical, mathematical, methodological, and computational development of descriptive statistics and exploratory data analysis.

The bibliography combines established statistical textbooks with openly accessible academic and institutional resources.

References will be associated with specific statistical procedures as the project develops.

The inclusion of a source in this preliminary bibliography does not imply that every chapter or statistical method contained in the publication has been implemented.

---

## 2. Core Statistical Textbooks

The following textbooks constitute the primary foundation for the project's statistical development.

- Measures of position.

## 6. Reference Selection Criteria

References must satisfy the following requirements:

1. Verifiable authorship.
2. Identifiable publication information.
3. Relevance to the statistical procedure.
4. Methodological reliability.
5. Appropriate conceptual coverage.
6. Clear distinction between open-access and restricted materials.

Bibliographic information will not be fabricated.

Specific page references will be incorporated after verification in the corresponding publication.

---

## 7. Bibliographic and Reproducibility Standards

The project follows APA 7th edition conventions.

Statistical methods will be documented using:

- Theoretical definitions.
- Mathematical expressions.
- Relevant bibliographic references.
- Software implementation details.
- Numerical validation procedures.
- Substantive interpretations.

Whenever different statistical conventions exist, the project will document the selected definition and its methodological justification.
